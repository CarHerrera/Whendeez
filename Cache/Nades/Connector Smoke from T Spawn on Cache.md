## Instructions
- You can start at the front of the blue shed and run, but it's easier to begin running right after spawning.
- Aim at the "O" between the "T" and "P" on the red sign, as shown.
- Run while holding W + D.
- When you reach the middle of the second window on the blue shed, jump and throw.
