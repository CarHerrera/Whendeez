## Instructions
This CS2 molotov lands on Mid from Mid on Anubis in Counter-Strike 2.

## How to throw

- Stand on Mid in the Corner next to Camera.
- Aim at top corner of the broken ceiling above the little Cave.
- Perform a running left click throw.
- The molotov will land on Mid on Anubis.
