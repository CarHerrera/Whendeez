## Instructions
This CS2 Molotov lands on Pillar from Street on Anubis in Counter-Strike 2.

## How to throw

- Stand on back Corner of Street near the Tunnel
- Aim at the bottom of the Stairs.
- Perform a left click walking jumpthrow.
- The smoke will land on Pillar on Anubis.
