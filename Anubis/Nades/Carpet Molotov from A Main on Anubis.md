## Instructions
This CS2 molotov lands on Carpet from A Main on Anubis in Counter-Strike 2.

## How to throw

- Stand in the corner next to the table in A Main.
- Aim at in the sky right next to the top Corner.
- Perform a left click throw.
- The molotov will land on Carpet on Anubis.
