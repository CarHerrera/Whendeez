## Instructions
This CS2 smoke lands on B Short from Back Alley on Mirage in Counter-Strike 2.

**How to throw**

- Position yourself in the corner at Back Alley.
- Aim at the left tip of the tower.
- Perform a left click throw.
- The smoke will land on B Short on Mirage.
