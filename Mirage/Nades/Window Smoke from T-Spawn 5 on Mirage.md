## Instructions
This CS2 smoke lands on Window from T-Spawn 5 on Mirage in Counter-Strike 2.

- For T-Spawn position 5.
- Aim at the balcony edge
- Perform a jumpthrow + W.
- The smoke will land on Window on Mirage.
