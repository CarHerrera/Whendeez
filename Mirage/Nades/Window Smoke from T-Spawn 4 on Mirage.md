## Instructions
This CS2 smoke lands on Window from T-Spawn 4 on Mirage in Counter-Strike 2.

## How to throw

- For T-Spawn position 4.
- Follow the white door frame down to the sandstone block and aim right below the first lip.
- Perform a jumpthrow + W.
- The smoke will land on Window on Mirage.
