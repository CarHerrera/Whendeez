## Instructions
This CS2 smoke lands on Window from T-Spawn 1 on Mirage in Counter-Strike 2.

## How to throw

- For T-Spawn location 1.
- Aim to the left of the window at the circular crack.
- Perform a jumpthrow + W.
- The smoke will land on Window on Mirage.
