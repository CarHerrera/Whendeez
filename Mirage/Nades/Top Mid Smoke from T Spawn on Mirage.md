## Instructions
This CS2 smoke lands on Top Mid from T Spawn on Mirage in Counter-Strike 2.

## How to throw

- Stand next to the trash can in T Spawn.
- Aim at the antenna.
- Perform a left click throw.
- The smoke will land on Top Mid on Mirage.
