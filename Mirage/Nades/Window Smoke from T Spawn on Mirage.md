## Instructions
This CS2 smoke lands on Window from T Spawn on Mirage in Counter-Strike 2.

## How to throw

- Stand on trashcan in T Spawn.
- Aim at the center of the edge, with the height of the doorframe to the left.
- Perform a crouching jumpthrow.
- The smoke will land on Window on Mirage.
