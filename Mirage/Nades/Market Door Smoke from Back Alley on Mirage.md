## Instructions
This CS2 smoke lands on Market Door from Back Alley on Mirage in Counter-Strike 2.

**How to throw**

- Position yourself in the corner of Back Alley.
- Aim at the top corner of the middle window on the right side of the tower.
- Perform a jumpthrow.
- The smoke will land on Market Door on Mirage.
