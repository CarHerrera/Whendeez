## Instructions
This CS2 smoke lands on Ticket Booth from T Spawn on Mirage in Counter-Strike 2.

## How to throw

- Stand against the slanted wall and adjust so you barely see the stone block petruding the ramp wall.
- Aim a tiny bit to the right of the wooden plank sticking out.
- Perform a jumpthrow.
- The nade has some leeway towards the left.
- The smoke will land on Ticket Booth on Mirage.
