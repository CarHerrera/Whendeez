## Instructions
This CS2 smoke lands on Ticket Booth from A Ramp Door on Mirage in Counter-Strike 2.

**How to throw**

- Line up with the right side of A Ramp Door.
- Aim on the bottom left corner of the brick that has darker edges.
- Perform a jumpthrow.
- The smoke will land on Ticket Booth on Mirage.
