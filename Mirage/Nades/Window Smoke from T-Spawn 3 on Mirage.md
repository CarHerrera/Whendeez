## Instructions
This CS2 smoke lands on Window from T-Spawn 3 on Mirage in Counter-Strike 2.

## How to throw

- Thrown from T Spawn location #3.
- Line up your crosshair where the vertical line of the buttefly in the left corner intersects with the horizontal line of the flower to the left of the butterfly.
- Perform a jumpthrow + W.
- The smoke will land on Window on Mirage.
