## Instructions
This CS2 smoke lands on Bottom Connector from T Spawn on Mirage in Counter-Strike 2.

**How to throw**

- Stand to the left side of the trash can in T Spawn.
- Locate the hanging carpet, in the middle rectagle towards the right bottom corner there's a pattern of zigzagging edges, aim at the same height as the 2nd one along the same vertical position as the first one.
- Perform a jumpthrow.
- The smoke will land on Bottom Connector on Mirage.
