## Instructions
Long Door Molly From Car(Needs to be done fast)

- Position at back left of car and jump on top
- Find lowest point of the white on the wall and aim halfway between it and the floor. Running jump throw
- Very Tricky to get the hang of but will make long control easier when mastered
