## Instructions
This CS2 smoke lands on CT Spawn from XBox on Dust 2 in Counter-Strike 2.

## How to throw

- Crouch in the corner at XBOX.
- Aim at the 5th board from the left on the right nail
- Perform a crouching jumpthrow.
- The smoke will land on CT Spawn on Dust 2.
