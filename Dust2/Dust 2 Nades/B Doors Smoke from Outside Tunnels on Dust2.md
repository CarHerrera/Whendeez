## Instructions
This CS2 smoke lands on B Doors from Outside Tunnels on Dust 2 in Counter-Strike 2.

## How to throw

- Stand at the outside tunnels corner by the wall
- Aim at the exposed brick.
- Perform a jumpthrow.
- The smoke will land on Doors on Dust 2.
