## Instructions
This CS2 smoke lands on B Window from Outside Tunnels on Dust 2 in Counter-Strike 2.

## How to throw

- Stand at the outside tunnels corner by the wall
- Aim at the dot.
- Perform a jumpthrow.
- The smoke will land on B Window on Dust 2.
