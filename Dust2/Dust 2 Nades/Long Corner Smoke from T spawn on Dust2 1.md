## Instructions
This CS2 smoke lands on Long Corner from T spawn on Dust 2 in Counter-Strike 2.

## How to throw

- Line up against corner of wall that leads to suicide.
- Aim directly above pipe coming out of the building.
- Perform a jumpthrow.
- The smoke will land on Long Corner on Dust 2.
