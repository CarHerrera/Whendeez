## Instructions
This CS2 smoke lands on Long Doors from Short Boost on Dust 2 in Counter-Strike 2.

## How to throw

- Position next to furthest box from elevator(cat boost boxes).
- Find pipe on the side of the building and aim where the bottom of the pipe ends.
- Perform a jumpthrow.
- The smoke will land on Long Doors on Dust 2.
